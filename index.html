What is CodecFake+?

CodecFake+ is a large-scale dataset for detecting deepfake speech produced by codec-based speech generation (CoSG) systems. Most CoSG systems are not publicly available, so CodecFake+ uses codec re-synthesized speech (CoRS) from 31 open-source neural audio codecs as proxy training data, and evaluates detectors on audio collected from 17 CoSG systems. A taxonomy of vector quantizer, auxiliary objective, and decoder type helps reveal which codec properties matter for detection.

How the Papers Relate

CodecFake+ is one part of our research line on codec-based deepfake speech. This table shows where it fits: the Dataset & Detection papers cover how CodecFake speech is detected and how well detectors generalize, and the Source Tracing papers use CodecFake+ to trace how a fake was generated, from defining the tasks to advancing the methods.
